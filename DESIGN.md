## Brand & Style

This design system articulates an authoritative, forward-looking ethos for international academic counselling and global credentials processing. Blending prestigious institutional heritage with the efficiency of modern mobility infrastructure, the aesthetic evokes global ambition, certified trust, and analytical rigor.

The visual direction merges **Corporate Precision** with **Technical Architectural Structure**. High-contrast typography pairs technical, wide-set geometric headlines with crisp, utilitarian body text to convey procedural accuracy. Structural interfaces favor micro-ruled borders, subtle dual-tone status tags, and intentional geometric compartmentalization over gratuitous soft styling. The atmosphere is that of an elite consular office or international university admissions bureau: clear, luminous, and unequivocally decisive.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate academic pedigree balanced by technical operational focus:

- **Primary (`#B45309` - Warm Oxford Amber)**: Deployed deliberately for active states, high-priority milestones, key interactive calls-to-action, credential highlights, and primary selection outlines.
- **Secondary (`#1A365D` - Deep Academic Navy)**: Represents institutional stability, structural headers, high-level navigational chrome, and dominant structural cards.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` - Verifier Teal)**: Reserved for verification chips, validated intake metrics, cross-border status verifications, and valid clearance stamps.
- **Neutral (`#475569` - Slate)**: Serves as the bedrock for calibrated body type, sub-borders, technical metadata, and low-contrast surface dividers.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Surface Canvas**: `#F8FAFC` (Crisp Slate Ice) maintains exceptional contrast without the stark sterility of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#FFFFFF` with precise `#E2E8F0` micro-borders for clean data panels and intake sections.
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#F1F5F9` for secondary modular wells, intake steps, and nested form groupings.
- **Surface Inverted / Authority**: `#0F172A` and `#1A365D` for modal heads, top navigation rails, and administrative oversight drawers.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts **Space Grotesk** with **Hanken Grotesk** to evoke the tension between modern logistical speed and authoritative institutional records:

- **Space Grotesk** provides an intentional, engineered look for headings, technical indicators, country code tags, role tabs, and data metrics. Its open geometry conveys progressive institutional thinking.
- **Hanken Grotesk** serves continuous copy, form inputs, regulatory criteria descriptions, and guidance notes. Its neutral grotesque cadence maximizes sustained legibility under dense informational conditions.

### Typographic Governance
- Headers maintain tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor screen hierarchy.
- Section tags, badge labels, and metadata markers must use `Space Grotesk` uppercase styling with expanded letter-spacing (`0.05em` to `0.08em`).
- Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced on numerical comparisons, tuition values, and visa progress counters.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured **12-column responsive grid** on desktop (`1280px` max fluid wrapper with explicit 8px baseline alignment) that gracefully transitions into modular cards on tablet and mobile viewports:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `2rem` gutters, and `3.5rem` outer page margins. Supports three-tier layouts: global navigational sidebar (260px fixed), primary intake dashboard (7-8 cols), and contextual analytical sidecar (4-5 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` page margins. Contextual panels collapse into toggleable contextual sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `0.75rem` gutters, and `1rem` margins. All segmented controls and form fields switch to fluid full-width blocks.

Vertical rhythm relies on uniform intervals using `space-md` (16px) for intra-component gaps and `space-xl` (40px) between discrete administrative records and document upload sections.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system communicates structural hierarchy through **calibrated tectonic borders combined with crisp, low-diffusion ambient offsets**, avoiding diffuse blur:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC` base background with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Ingestion Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` background bound by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State)**: Applied to hover states, focused record lines, and open selectors. Retains 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) accented by a deep-navy tinted drop: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Dossier Overlay)**: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` with a 1px micro-border of `#94A3B8`.
- **Active Focus Ring**: `0 0 0 3px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.22)`, providing unambiguous accessible guidance across high-density intake fields.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **precision-rounded structural grammar** (Level 2):

- **Core Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Buttons, form controls, role selector toggles, validation alert banners, and table rows.
- **Structural Containers (`1rem` / 16px)**: Primary application cards, verified university panels, submission cards, and dialog bodies.
- **Full Shell / Modals (`1.5rem` / 24px)**: Outer frames for global application drawers, slide-overs, and onboarding containers.
- **Badges & Role Indicators**: Strict `9999px` pill contours for operational status indicators (e.g., "PASSED", "VISA LODGED", "UNDER REVIEW"), establishing immediate distinction against architectural content blocks.

## Components

### Role Selector (Segmented Control)
- Constructed with a `#F1F5F9` slate-recessed track, 4px internal padding, and 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Segments (Student, Counsellor, Administrator) feature `Space Grotesk` font (`label-md`).
- Active segment: `#FFFFFF` surface, high-contrast `#1A365D` text, subtle elevation shadow, and a 2px bottom accent in `#B45309`. Inactive segments show `#64748B` neutral type with gentle hover brightening.

### Role Badges & Status Chips
- **Student**: Tinted `#EFF6FF` background, `#1D4ED8` border and typography.
- **Counsellor**: Tinted `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` border and typography.
- **Administrator**: Tinted `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` border and typography.
- **Verified Status**: Pill-shaped container, `#0D9488` text with `#CCFBF1` background and a prefix checkmark glyph.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `#B45309` solid fill, white text, 8px radius, with subtle active-state compression (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary (Institutional)**: Deep navy `#1A365D` fill with crisp white text for procedural completions, PDF endorsements, and official sign-offs.
- **Outline / Administrative**: White background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#1E293B` text; on hover transitions to `#F8FAFC` with a `#B45309` border.

### Accessible Input Fields & Validation System
- Input field boundaries utilize a structural 1.5px border (`#CBD5E1`), high-contrast placeholder type (`#94A3B8`), and generous 12px vertical padding.
- **Success State**: 1.5px `#0D9488` border, accompanied by an inline check icon and positive validation metadata in `body-sm`.
- **Error State**: 1.5px `#DC2626` border, subtle `#FEF2F2` inner wash, accompanied by an explicit contextual recovery message below the baseline.
- **Active Focus**: Crisp `#B45309` perimeter border with an outer `3px` focus ring tinted at 20% opacity.

### Document & Application Dossier Cards
- White surface bounded by 1px `#E2E8F0` framing.
- Header line features a secondary navy badge alongside an amber step-tracker.
- Built-in drag-and-drop ingestion zones with technical dashed lines (`2px dashed #CBD5E1`) transitioning to solid `#B45309` under file drag focus.
